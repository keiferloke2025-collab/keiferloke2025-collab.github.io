About the star

Born in Akron, Ohio, LeBron James grew up with a passion for basketball and quickly became a national sensation in high school. He entered the NBA as the number one overall pick in 2003 and transformed the game with his combination of size, speed, and vision. Over his career, he has become a four-time NBA champion, four-time MVP, and a global icon who inspires players and fans around the world.

Beyond basketball, James is known for his leadership, philanthropy, and strong voice in sports and culture. His impact reaches far beyond the court, making him one of the most influential athletes in modern history.
